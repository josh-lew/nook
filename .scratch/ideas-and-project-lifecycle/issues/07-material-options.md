# 07: Material Options on Ideas and Projects

**What to build:** The maker can add, edit and remove Material Options (a link and/or screenshot with an optional comment) on Ideas and on Projects at any Stage, and mark one or more as chosen on a Project.

- [ ] Several Material Options can be marked chosen on a Project; chosen ones are visually distinct
- [ ] Data-layer tests cover update of the chosen flag and removal
